Responsible AI, Fairness & Limitations
Model Limitations
Data Leakage Risk

Features like Risk_Score are derived from target-correlated variables. In production, ensure no future information leaks into training features.

Class Imbalance

SMOTE was applied to handle imbalance. However, synthetic samples may not perfectly represent real-world minority class distribution.

Temporal Drift

Model performance may degrade over time as economic conditions change. Regular retraining is recommended.

Encoded Variables

Employment_Type and City are numerically encoded. Ordinal encoding may introduce unintended relationships.

Fairness & Ethics
Bias Monitoring

Monitor model predictions across demographic groups (age, employment type) to detect disparate impact. Regularly audit for protected attribute bias.

Explainability

Feature importance provides model transparency. For production use, consider SHAP values for individual prediction explanations (ECOA compliance).

Human Oversight

Model predictions should augment, not replace, human credit officers. Borderline cases (40–60% probability) require manual review.

Data Privacy

Customer IDs are anonymized. In production, ensure PII is handled per GDPR/local data protection regulations.
